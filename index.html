01 · The edge

What is +EV betting?

Most bettors pick who they think will win. +EV bettors ask a different question: is this price wrong? Every set of odds implies a probability. When a sportsbook's odds imply a lower chance than the real one, the bet is worth more than it costs, and over enough bets that gap turns into profit.

1

Odds are probabilities

+150 implies a 40% chance. −200 implies 66.7%. Books bake in extra margin (the "vig") so the implied odds on both sides add up to more than 100%.

2

Find the fair line

Sharp, high-limit markets are the most accurate prices out there. Strip out their vig and you get a fair, no-vig probability for each outcome.

3

Bet the mispriced side

When another book or DFS app offers better odds than the fair line, that's +EV. You won't win every bet, but you're getting paid more than the risk is worth.

Example

The coin flip

A fair coin lands heads 50% of the time, so fair odds are +100. If someone offers you +110 on heads, you'll still lose about half your bets, but each win pays $110 while each loss costs only $100.

Win 50% ×+$110
Lose 50% ×−$100
EV per $100 bet+$5.00
